# French Learning App

This app supports one person in learning French, with a focus on using vocabulary and forming sentences in speech and writing.

## Language

**Learner**:
The single person who uses the app to develop their French skills.

**Collection**:
A named grouping of French vocabulary items, including words and expressions, that the learner can use to focus practice. The learner can create collections and add or edit their vocabulary items and definitions.

**Flashcard**:
A study item for a French word, expression, or idiom, paired with its English translation and supporting information.

**Practice question**:
A prompt that asks the learner to recall vocabulary or construct a French sentence.

**Curated question**:
A practice question included with a built-in vocabulary collection or grammar concept.

**Answer key**:
The correct answer and any accepted alternatives for a practice question, established when the question is created.

**Distractor**:
An incorrect answer option in a multiple-choice question.

**Attempt**:
One answered quiz question, kept with its correctness, time, session, and collection or grammar concept. Repeating a question makes a new attempt.

**General quiz**:
A short quiz across vocabulary and grammar that begins with the questions of highest review priority.

**Focused quiz**:
A quiz on one collection or grammar concept chosen by the learner.

**Review priority**:
The relative order in which questions are worth reviewing. It is never a due date or a mastery level.
