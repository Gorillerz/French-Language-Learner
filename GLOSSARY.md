# French Learning App

This app supports one person in learning French, with a focus on using vocabulary and forming sentences in speech and writing.

## Language

**Learner**:
The single person who uses the app to develop their French skills.

**Collection**:
A named grouping of French vocabulary items, including words and expressions, that the learner can use to focus practice. The learner can create collections and add or edit their vocabulary items and definitions.

**Topic**:
A collection or grammar concept: what questions, ratings and results are grouped by.

**Flashcard**:
A study item for a French word, expression, or idiom, paired with its English translation and supporting information.

**Sentence flashcard**:
A study item for one sentence of a grammar concept, asking for its translation in one direction and then for a self-rating. Each direction is rated separately.

**Practice question**:
A prompt that asks the learner to recall vocabulary or construct a French sentence.

**Curated question**:
A practice question included with a built-in vocabulary collection or grammar concept.

**Generated question**:
A practice question written by AI at the learner's request, in a batch of exactly 10 for one collection or grammar concept. Each has an explanation for every option.

**Answer key**:
The correct answer and any accepted alternatives for a practice question, established when the question is created.

**Distractor**:
An incorrect answer option in a multiple-choice question.

**Attempt**:
One answered quiz question, kept with its correctness, time, session, and collection or grammar concept. Repeating a question makes a new attempt.

**General quiz**:
A short quiz across vocabulary and grammar that begins with the questions of highest review priority.

**Focused quiz**:
A quiz on one collection or grammar concept chosen by the learner.

**Quiz review**:
A look back over the attempts of one quiz, showing the correct and chosen answers and, for generated questions, the explanations. It is the only place explanations appear.

**Passphrase**:
The long secret, chosen by the learner, that unlocks synced learning data on each device. It never leaves a device and it is not the sign-in password.

**Recovery code**:
A generated code, saved by the learner, that sets a new passphrase when the old one is forgotten.

**Sign-in**:
The ordinary account that identifies the learner to the sync service. Resetting it cannot restore encrypted data.

**Topic help**:
Questions the learner asks AI about one flashcard or grammar concept, outside any quiz. The answers are not saved.

**Follow-up question**:
A question the learner asks AI about one quiz question during quiz review. The answers are not saved.

**Review priority**:
The relative order in which questions are worth reviewing. It is never a due date or a mastery level.
