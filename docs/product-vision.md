# French Learning App — Product Vision

This document captures the long-term capabilities the app may grow into. It is product context, not a commitment that every feature belongs in the first version. The first-version plan should support the learner’s priorities while keeping these future needs visible.

## Learner and product shape

- Private, single-user app for the author, currently around B2–C1 in French.
- Long-term goal: broad French fluency, especially retrieving vocabulary and constructing correct, natural sentences.
- Responsive, installable Progressive Web App for Windows desktop and Android.
- Shared learning progress synchronized through a cloud database.
- Local processing where practical; offline functionality may come later.
- AI features use LLM APIs through OpenRouter.

## Vocabulary and flashcards

- Create custom cards for words, phrases, expressions, and sentences.
- Provide predefined decks organized by frequency, topic, and difficulty.
- Store definitions, translations, examples, grammatical gender, and usage notes.
- Use spaced repetition for long-term memorization.
- Support AI-assisted card creation and explanations.

## Grammar learning

- Offer a comprehensive French grammar curriculum from A1 to C2.
- Explain rules, examples, exceptions, and comparisons with English.
- Generate ongoing practice in varied formats: translation, fill-in-the-blank, error correction, and sentence construction.
- Adapt difficulty, including genuinely challenging exercises.
- Revisit grammatical concepts through spaced repetition and varied questions.

## Reading comprehension

- Provide French stories, articles, and other texts at different difficulty levels.
- Let the learner select words or expressions to see definitions, translations, and explanations.
- Save unfamiliar vocabulary directly to flashcards.
- Offer comprehension quizzes and vocabulary practice based on each text.
- Generate reading material on selected topics with AI.

## Writing practice and correction

- Support free writing, journals, essays, and daily prompts.
- Analyze grammar, spelling, punctuation, vocabulary, and style with AI.
- Highlight mistakes and unnatural expressions inline.
- Explain corrections interactively and summarize errors below each submission.
- Identify and classify recurring weaknesses.
- Turn writing mistakes into future practice.

## Unified quiz and practice

- Provide a general “Quiz Me” flow combining vocabulary, grammar, reading, and previous mistakes.
- Support multiple choice and written answers, plus other question formats.
- Personalize questions using weaknesses and learning history.
- Adapt difficulty and use spaced repetition.
- Support focused topic practice and mixed quizzes.
- Make AI-generated question supply effectively unlimited in normal use.

## AI language tutor

- Make contextual AI help available throughout the app.
- Answer questions about grammar, vocabulary, expressions, and mistakes.
- Explain why answers are right or wrong.
- Generate examples, exercises, and alternative phrasing.
- Connect to LLM providers through OpenRouter.

## Progress and learning analytics

- Show vocabulary retention, grammar mastery, and exercise performance.
- Track correct and incorrect answers and recurring mistakes by topic or grammatical concept.
- Visualize progress over time.
- Recommend what to learn or review next.
- Provide a knowledge map of mastered concepts, weak areas, and topics due for review.

## Future operating needs

- Keep one learning history available across Windows and Android.
- Prefer local processing where practical.
- Consider offline functionality as a later capability.
